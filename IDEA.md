An adaptive daily planner that reshapes your schedule fluidly around live context, energy, and interruptions.

- Reprioritize tasks dynamically when plans change mid-day (meetings, delays, fatigue).
- Learn personal productivity rhythms to predict optimal windows for deep vs. shallow work.
- Accept one-word micro-input via CLI or messaging (e.g., "start", "break") with near-zero friction.
- Offer a calm, water-inspired text UI that visually slides tasks like flowing streams, not rigid blocks.
